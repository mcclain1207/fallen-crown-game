How to play

Each person gets a secret character. At night, close your eyes while the host runs abilities. During the day, discuss and vote to banish a suspect. The Loyalists win by banishing the Usurper. The Traitors win when living Evil players equal or outnumber living Good players.

Banished players may keep discussing and get one final vote for the rest of the game. The host tracks that vote on paper. Do not show your private role card unless your host allows it.

Host note: This version handles live joining, secret roles, phases, and banishment. Night actions and votes are moderated in person, not automated.
